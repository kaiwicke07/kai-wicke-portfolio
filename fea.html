Plane Stress FEA 2026

A 2D finite element solver that runs in the browser. You draw a part, add supports and loads, and it shows the deformed shape and the stress.

I wrote the finite element core from scratch, with no FEA or linear algebra libraries.

Live siteCode

What it does

Parts
A cantilever, a tension strip, a plate with a hole, or a part you draw yourself with holes, snapping and undo.
Supports and loads
Fixed supports or rollers, and forces, pressures or tractions, on any edge, hole edges included.
Elements
3-node constant strain triangles and 6-node triangles with curved edges.
Meshing
Structured meshes for the presets, and my own Delaunay mesher for drawn parts, finer near holes.
Results
Von Mises and other stresses, the deformed shape, a factor of safety and an equilibrium check on every solve.
Speed
The solver runs in a background thread, so the page stays responsive while it works.

Checked against the textbook

A validation page reruns every study in your browser with the same code and tolerances as the automated tests.
