Autonomous car 2025

A small car that drives itself. Three ultrasonic sensors measure the distance to the walls, and it steers away from obstacles.

I built the chassis, the wiring and the code.

How it works

  1. Sensing. The three sensors measure the distance ahead and to each side.
  2. Deciding. The Arduino compares the distances and turns toward whichever side has more room.
  3. Driving. A motor driver board sets the wheel motors’ speeds to steer.

Built with

Arduino, C++, ultrasonic sensors, a motor driver and an acrylic chassis.
