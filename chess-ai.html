chess.ai 2026

A chess trainer that learns from your own games. It imports your Lichess or Chess.com games, analyses every move with Stockfish in your browser, and turns your mistakes into puzzles.

It costs nothing to run: no accounts, no paid services, and your games never leave your browser.

Live siteCode

What it does

Game review
An evaluation bar and graph, the engine’s best line, and every inaccuracy, mistake and blunder marked.
Puzzles
Positions from your own mistakes, kept only when one move is clearly best.
Spaced repetition
Puzzles come back on a schedule, sooner if you got them wrong.
Openings
A tree of your openings, ranked by where you lose the most.
Bots and a coach
Six bots from about 400 to 2400. The coach pauses the game after a bad move and lets you try again.
Progress
Accuracy, blunder rate and puzzle results week by week.

How it works

  1. Import. The browser fetches your games straight from Lichess or Chess.com.
  2. Analyse. Stockfish runs in the browser. Positions are cached, so an opening shared by many games is only analysed once.
  3. Judge. Mistakes are graded with Lichess’s own rules. A test checks my version against Lichess’s verdicts on real games.
  4. Explain. Notes like “this hangs the bishop” come from replaying the engine’s lines, not from guesses.
  5. Practise. Mistakes become puzzles, and everything is scheduled for review.

Built with

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, chess.js, Stockfish (WebAssembly), IndexedDB, Vitest.
