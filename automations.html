        How a job goes

        To begin, you email me a few lines about what you do by hand every week and which tools you use. I reply the same day with a plan and a fixed price, or tell you honestly that it isn't worth automating.

        Within a week, I build it and run it on sample data, then send you a short screen recording of it working. Nothing touches your real customers yet.

        When you're happy, we connect it to your real accounts together, and you get written notes on how it works and how to switch it off.

        From then on, it sends me an alert if a step fails. Fixes are free for 30 days, and after that the care plan covers them.

        Questions

        Is my customer data safe?

        It stays in your own accounts. Everything runs on your Google, Microsoft or Zapier account, with only the access each step needs, and you can remove my access the day we finish.

        What tools do you work with?

        Zapier, Make and n8n; Gmail, Outlook and Google Sheets; Airtable, HubSpot, Square, Shopify, Calendly, Stripe, Twilio and Slack; Claude and OpenAI when a step needs reading or writing. If a tool has an API or an export, it can usually be connected. If it can't, I'll say so before you pay.

        Do I have to pay for AI?

        Usually not. Most of the work is plain rules. Where AI helps, it costs a few dollars a month on your own account.

        Do I own what you build?

        Yes. It's built in your accounts, and you get the notes and any code.

        Can we hire you on Upwork?

        Yes, if you'd rather pay through Upwork's protection. Email me and I'll send the link, at the same prices.
