Questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome whenever we are open. Booking just holds a chair for you at a set quarter hour, which helps on Saturdays.

Why quarter hours?

Every service fits in 15, 30 or 45 minutes, so the schedule never drifts. You sit down at the time on your booking, not twenty minutes after it.

What if I'm running late?

Call us. Up to ten minutes late we can usually still do the full service. Past that we move you to the next free quarter.

How do I pay?

Card, tap, cash or Venmo. Tips can go on the card. No deposit to book.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, any age. For first haircuts book a 30-minute slot on a weekday morning, when the shop is quiet.

How do I get there?

We are on Saratoga Street, a short walk from Day Square and about ten minutes from Maverick on the Blue Line. Street parking is easiest on weekday mornings.
