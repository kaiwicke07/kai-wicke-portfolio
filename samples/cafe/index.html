Out of the oven today

One oven means one tray at a time. These are the bakes, in order; the highlighted one is next out.

    A bakery the size of a thimble

    Thimble Oven is 340 square feet, one deck oven and a counter with six stools, two doors down from Roslindale Square.

    Small on purpose

    The oven holds one tray at a time, so we bake little and often. Croissants come out at 6:30, bread at 8, focaccia late morning. When a tray sells out, the next one is usually forty minutes away.

    Coffee to match

    Espresso from a small roaster in Somerville, pulled on a two-group machine. Drip is brewed in small pots so it never sits. Bring a mug and we take fifty cents off.
