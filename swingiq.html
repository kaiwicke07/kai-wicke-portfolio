SwingIQ 2026

A free golf swing analyser. Film a swing on your phone and it tracks your body frame by frame, measures your swing, tells you the one thing to fix first and gives you drills for it.

Your video is analysed on your own device. Nothing is uploaded and there’s no account.

Live siteCode

What it measures

Tempo
Backswing time over downswing time. Good players are close to 3 to 1.
Posture
Forward bend at address, and whether you stand up through impact.
Head and hips
Head rising, dipping or sliding, and hips swaying in the backswing.
Arms and turn
Lead arm bend at the top, and an estimate of your shoulder turn.
Balance
Whether you finish with your weight on your lead foot.
Drills
Each fault maps to step-by-step drills from a 14-drill library.

How it works

  1. Tracking. MediaPipe pose tracking finds 33 body points in up to 240 frames of the video.
  2. Cleaning. Shaky or low-confidence points are filled in and smoothed.
  3. Phases. It finds address, the top, impact and the finish from how fast the hands move.
  4. Measuring. Angles and distances are scaled to your own body, so the distance to the camera doesn’t matter.
  5. Coaching. A priority order picks the first thing to fix, so you aren’t given ten things at once.

Built with

React, TypeScript, Vite, MediaPipe pose tracking, Vitest with real swings as test cases.
