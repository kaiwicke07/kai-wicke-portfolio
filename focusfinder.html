FocusFinder 2026

A desk robot that notices when you stop paying attention. A pair of animated eyes follows you, and if you look away for too long it nudges you back.

How it works

  1. Seeing. A Raspberry Pi Zero 2W runs OpenCV face detection on a camera feed.
  2. Eyes. Animated eyes on a transparent OLED screen move to follow where your face is.
  3. Feedback. If you look away for too long, a Raspberry Pi Pico turns on an LED and a buzzer.
  4. Timer. A 20-minute Pomodoro timer and an RGB light help you work in focused blocks.

Built with

Raspberry Pi Zero 2W, Raspberry Pi Pico, OpenCV, Python and MicroPython, a camera, a transparent OLED, an RGB LED and a buzzer.
