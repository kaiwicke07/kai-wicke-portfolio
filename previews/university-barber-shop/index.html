Down the steps since 1924

Look for the red sign over the door and the barber pole on Commonwealth Avenue. The shop is one flight down.

University Barber Shop is independently family-owned and has been cutting hair since 1924. It keeps an old-school feel on purpose: the kind of barber shop a father took his son to. Barbers here have served Boston University students, faculty and staff for generations, and there are televisions on for the game while you wait. Bring a new style or keep the one you have: business cuts, fades and the latest looks.

Prices

A haircut is $30, paid in cash. The neck shave with a straight razor is on the house.

The shop also sells American Crew and Paul Mitchell hair products to take home.
