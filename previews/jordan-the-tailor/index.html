Bring it in.
Leave it fitted.

Clothes off the rack rarely fit like they should. This is a Newbury Street workroom for getting them right.

  • Wedding gowns

    Gowns are a speciality of the shop. Bring yours in and talk through the fit you want for the day.

  • Alterations

    Sewing and alterations for the clothes you already own, so they fit you instead of the hanger.

  • Expert tailoring

    Cut and sewn to your exact measurements, after questions about how you want each piece to fit.

  • Attention to detail

    Personal attention on every piece, from the first fitting to the last stitch.

    Not sure if a garment can be fixed? Call and ask.

How a fitting goes

  • You're sized up at a glance: proportions first.
  • Then the questions: how you want it to sit, where, and how close.
  • Then it's cut and sewn to your measurements.
