Half shop, half cafe

About 1,000 square feet with a 1990s look: VHS-era visuals and retro displays.

On the racks: carefully sourced vintage and streetwear, sportswear, designer pieces, sneakers and collectibles. At the counter: espresso, brewed coffee and lattes made with beans from Onyx Coffee Lab.

Stock turns over constantly, so the newest finds go up on Instagram first.

“I like the idea of a hybrid cafe because they don’t really exist around here.”

Owner Philip Kahan, to The Boston Globe, July 2026

Four bedrooms of clothes

Owner Philip Kahan spent years in Los Angeles making music before coming home to New England.

He had already run a vintage shop, High Society in Portland, built around his collection of vintage tees. It closed in 2022. Back in Massachusetts, he looked for a storefront for more than a year and chose Somerville for its “steady foot traffic and neighborhood feel.”

The collection came too. “I had four bedrooms filled of just my clothes,” he told the Globe.
